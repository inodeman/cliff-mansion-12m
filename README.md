## English

**Title**

$12 Million Cliff Mansion | Full Construction Timelapse + Tour

**Description**

This cliff was empty at sunrise. Eighteen months later it holds a $12 million house that should not be able to stand here. Full construction timelapse, then a one-minute tour.

Watch the whole build: first stakes, the crew arriving, excavation on the headland, rebar and cliff anchors, the concrete pour, carpenters taking the frame vertical, the glass wall, and the finished house at dusk.

This is a cinematic visualization of a cliffside build, not footage of a real permitted project.

## Espanol

**Titulo**

Mansión de $12 Millones en un Acantilado | Timelapse + Recorrido

Es una visualización cinematográfica de una obra en acantilado, no el registro de un proyecto real con permiso.
